Add AI Intelligence

Elevate your app from a simple database to a smart tool.

🤖

Smart Assistant

AppSheet can auto-suggest actions based on user behavior. Enable in Settings → Intelligence.

📷

OCR (Text from Images)

Users can snap a photo and the app reads text from it. Great for receipts, labels, or documents.

📊

Predictive Models

AppSheet can learn patterns from your data and make predictions (e.g., price estimates).

Start simple: You don't need ALL of these features. Pick the ONE that makes the biggest difference for your users. You can always add more later.

Workshop Exercise

Build on Google Sites

Step-by-step: from blank page to published landing page.

  • 1 Go to sites.google.com
  • 2 Click "Blank" to create a new site
  • 3 Add a hero section with your headline
  • 4 Add a text section with your 3 features
  • 5 Add a button linking to your AppSheet app
  • 6 Click "Publish" in top right
  • 7 Choose a custom URL name
  • ✓ Your landing page is LIVE!

Landing Page Checklist

  • ✅ Clear headline — what does the app do?
  • ✅ 3 feature bullets — why should I care?
  • ✅ Call-to-action button — try the app!
  • ✅ Your app link — connects to AppSheet
  • ✅ Mobile-friendly — test on phone!

Write Your Launch Message

Use Gemini to craft the perfect message for sharing your app.

WhatsApp Launch Message
Write a WhatsApp message to share my new app with friends. App: [APP NAME] What it does: [ONE LINE] Link: [YOUR LINK] Requirements: - Short (max 5 lines) - Casual and friendly tone - Include the link - Ask them to try it and give feedback - Add 1-2 relevant emojis
Twitter/X Launch Post
Write a tweet announcing my new app. App: [APP NAME] What it does: [ONE LINE] Link: [YOUR LINK] Requirements: - Under 280 characters - Exciting but authentic - Include link - Include 2-3 hashtags - Sound like a real person, not a company

Post-Launch

Collect Feedback

Your first users are your best teachers. Listen carefully.

Feedback Survey Prompt
Create a short feedback survey for my app [APP NAME]. 5 questions only: 1. Did you find it easy to use? (1-5 scale) 2. What did you like most? 3. What was confusing or frustrating? 4. Would you use it again? Why? 5. What ONE feature would you add? Format as a Google Form I can create quickly.

What to Do with Feedback

  • 1 Don't take it personally — criticism = growth
  • 2 Look for patterns — if 3+ people say the same thing, fix it
  • 3 Quick wins first — fix easy problems immediately
  • 4 Thank every user — they're helping you build
  • 5 Share improvements — "You asked, we built it!"
