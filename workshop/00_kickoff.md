## What is OusiaWorld?

OusiaWorld is a **4-week hands-on program** where you will go from **zero to launching your own app** — without writing a single line of code.

You will use **AI and free Google tools** to:
- Find real problems worth solving
- Build an intelligent app that helps people
- Launch it to real users

**This is not a lecture series.** This is a builder program. You watch, you prompt, you build.

---

## How the Program Works

### Pre-Study (Before the Workshop)
1. **Watch the video** for the week (5-10 minutes each)
2. **Follow along** with the prompts and templates provided
3. **Try it yourself** on your phone or computer

### Workshop (Live Session)
1. **Practice together** — hands-on building with support
2. **Share your progress** and get feedback from peers
3. **Complete the Action task** for the week

## What You Need to Get Started

- [ ] A **Google account** (Gmail)
- [ ] A **smartphone or computer** with internet access
- [ ] Access to [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com)
- [ ] Willingness to **try, fail, and try again**

That's it. No coding experience. No design skills. No business degree.

---

## Your Mindset for This Program

### Be a Builder, Not a Student
You are not here to memorize theory. You are here to **build something real** that solves a problem for real people.

### Embrace "Good Enough"
Your first app will not be perfect. That's the point. **Ship fast, learn fast, improve fast.**

### Help Each Other
The best ideas come from collaboration. Share your work, give feedback, and learn from your peers in the OusiaWorld community.

### Think Local, Act Now
The best problems to solve are the ones **you see every day**. Lagos traffic. Campus frustrations. Market inefficiencies. Your lived experience is your superpower.
