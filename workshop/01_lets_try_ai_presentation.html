Step 3

The Art of Prompting

AI is like a very smart assistant who needs clear instructions. Master these 3 rules.

01

Give Context

Tell AI who you are, where you are, and what you're working on.

Give me app ideas
I am a student at University of Lagos. Give me 5 app ideas for campus problems.
02

Be Specific

Tell AI exactly what format and detail level you want.

Tell me about ride-sharing
Explain ride-sharing in 3 bullet points. Simple English. Focus on African cities.
03

Iterate

Don't stop at the first answer. Keep the conversation going.

Great list! Let's focus on #3. Who would use it? What are the top 3 features?

Exercise 1 ⏱ 10 minutes

Problem Discovery

Follow this 3-step prompting sequence. Copy-paste each one into Gemini.

Prompt A

Discover Problems

I am a university student in [YOUR CITY], Nigeria. List 10 frustrating daily problems that students like me face. Focus on: transportation, food, studying, or money.
Prompt B

Go Deeper

Let's focus on [PROBLEM YOU CHOSE]. - Why does this problem exist? - Who suffers from it most? - How do people currently solve it? - What would an ideal solution look like?
Prompt C

Shape the App

Based on our discussion about [PROBLEM], help me define a simple app: 1. App Name (catchy) 2. One-line description 3. Target user (specific) 4. Top 3 features only

Exercise 2 ⏱ 10 minutes

Elevator Pitch

Turn your app idea into a compelling 30-second pitch.

Pitch Generator Prompt
COPY
Help me write a 30-second elevator pitch for my app. App Name: [YOUR APP NAME] Problem: [THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES] Target User: [WHO USES IT] Key Feature: [THE MAIN THING IT DOES] The pitch should be: - Maximum 4 sentences - Simple enough for my grandmother to understand - Exciting enough for an investor to want to learn more

Pro Tip: Read the pitch out loud. If it takes more than 30 seconds, ask Gemini: "Make it shorter and punchier."

Exercise 3 ⏱ 10 minutes

Explore AI Capabilities

AI can do much more than answer questions. Try each of these superpowers.

🎭 Make AI Play a Role
You are a successful Nigerian tech entrepreneur. I just told you my app idea: [YOUR APP IDEA]. Give me honest feedback. What's good? What's risky? What should I focus on first?
✍️ Make AI Write for You
Write a short social media post (for Twitter/X) announcing my new app. App Name: [NAME] What it does: [DESCRIPTION] Make it exciting and include 2 relevant hashtags.
📋 Make AI Think Step-by-Step
I want to build [YOUR APP IDEA] using only free Google tools. Break down the entire process into 10 simple steps. Assume I have zero coding experience.

✓ AI Can Help You

  • 💡 Brainstorm ideas
  • 🔍 Analyze problems
  • ✍️ Write content
  • 📋 Plan step-by-step
  • 🎭 Play different roles (advisor, critic, customer)

✗ What NOT to Do

  • ⚠️ Don't trust everything blindly — always check facts
  • ⚠️ Don't copy-paste without reading — make it yours
  • ⚠️ Don't ask vague questions — be specific

AI is a tool, not magic.
The quality of the output depends on the quality of your input.
