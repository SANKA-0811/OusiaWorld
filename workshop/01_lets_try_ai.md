# Let's Try AI! - Hands-On Guide

## Your First Conversation with AI

Today, you will have your first real conversation with **Gemini**, Google's AI assistant. By the end of this session, you will know how to:

1. Talk to AI effectively (prompting)
2. Use AI to brainstorm real ideas
3. Refine AI outputs to get exactly what you need

---

## Step 1: Open Gemini

1. Open your browser (Chrome recommended)
2. Go to **gemini.google.com**
3. Sign in with your Google account
4. You're ready!

---

## Step 2: Your First Prompt

Let's start simple. Type this into Gemini:

```
Hello! I am a university student in Nigeria.
I want to build an app that solves a real problem.
Can you suggest 5 daily problems that Nigerian students face?
```

**What to notice:**
- Gemini gives you a list immediately
- The answers are relevant because you gave **context** (who you are, where you are)
- This is the power of a good prompt: **context + clear request = useful answer**

---

## Step 3: The Art of Prompting

AI is like a very smart assistant who needs clear instructions. Here are three rules:

### Rule 1: Give Context (Who, Where, What)

| Bad Prompt | Good Prompt |
|-----------|------------|
| "Give me app ideas" | "I am a student at the University of Lagos. Give me 5 app ideas that solve daily problems for students on campus." |

### Rule 2: Be Specific About the Output

| Bad Prompt | Good Prompt |
|-----------|------------|
| "Tell me about ride-sharing" | "Explain ride-sharing apps in 3 bullet points. Use simple English. Focus on how they work in African cities." |

### Rule 3: Iterate — Ask Follow-Up Questions

Don't stop at the first answer. Keep the conversation going:

```
That's a great list! Let's focus on idea #3.
Who exactly would use this app?
What are the top 3 features it needs?
```

## Hands-On Exercise 1: Problem Discovery (10 min)

Try these prompts one by one. Copy-paste them into Gemini:

### Prompt A: Discover Problems
```
I am a university student in [YOUR CITY], Nigeria.
List 10 frustrating daily problems that students like me face.
Focus on problems related to: transportation, food, studying, or money.
```

### Prompt B: Go Deeper
Pick the problem that resonates with you most, then ask:
```
Let's focus on [PROBLEM YOU CHOSE].
- Why does this problem exist?
- Who suffers from it the most?
- How do people currently try to solve it?
- What would an ideal solution look like?
```

### Prompt C: Shape the App Idea
```
Based on our discussion about [PROBLEM], help me define a simple app:
1. App Name (something catchy)
2. One-line description
3. Target user (be specific)
4. Top 3 features only (keep it simple!)
```

## Hands-On Exercise 2: Elevator Pitch (10 min)

Now let's turn your idea into a pitch. Use this prompt:

```
Help me write a 30-second elevator pitch for my app.

App Name: [YOUR APP NAME]
Problem: [THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES]
Target User: [WHO USES IT]
Key Feature: [THE MAIN THING IT DOES]

The pitch should be:
- Maximum 4 sentences
- Simple enough for my grandmother to understand
- Exciting enough for an investor to want to learn more
```

**Pro Tip:** Read the pitch out loud. If it takes more than 30 seconds, ask Gemini to make it shorter.

## Hands-On Exercise 3: Explore AI Capabilities (10 min)

Let's see what else AI can do. Try each of these:

### Make AI Play a Role
```
You are a successful Nigerian tech entrepreneur.
I just told you my app idea: [YOUR APP IDEA].
Give me honest feedback. What's good? What's risky?
What should I focus on first?
```

### Make AI Write for You
```
Write a short social media post (for Twitter/X) announcing my new app.
App Name: [YOUR APP NAME]
What it does: [ONE LINE DESCRIPTION]
Make it exciting and include 2 relevant hashtags.
```

### Make AI Think Step-by-Step
```
I want to build [YOUR APP IDEA] using only free Google tools.
Break down the entire process into 10 simple steps.
Assume I have zero coding experience.
```

## Key Takeaways

### What You Learned Today

1. **AI is a tool, not magic.** The quality of the output depends on the quality of your input (prompt).

2. **Three rules of prompting:**
   - Give context (who, where, what)
   - Be specific about the output you want
   - Iterate — keep asking follow-up questions

3. **AI can help you:**
   - Brainstorm ideas
   - Analyze problems
   - Write content
   - Plan step-by-step
   - Play different roles (advisor, critic, customer)

### What NOT to Do with AI

- Don't trust everything blindly — always check facts
- Don't copy-paste without reading — make it yours
- Don't ask vague questions — be specific

## Quick Reference: Prompt Templates

Save these templates for later use:

### Brainstorming Template
```
I am [WHO YOU ARE] in [WHERE YOU ARE].
List [NUMBER] ideas for [WHAT YOU WANT].
Focus on [SPECIFIC AREA/CONSTRAINT].
```

### Feedback Template
```
You are a [ROLE/EXPERT].
Here is my idea: [YOUR IDEA]
Give me:
- 3 strengths
- 3 weaknesses
- 1 suggestion to improve it
```

### Writing Template
```
Write a [TYPE OF CONTENT] about [TOPIC].
Audience: [WHO WILL READ IT]
Tone: [FORMAL/CASUAL/EXCITING]
Length: [SHORT/MEDIUM/LONG]
```

### Step-by-Step Template
```
I want to [GOAL].
Break it down into [NUMBER] simple steps.
Assume I have [EXPERIENCE LEVEL].
Each step should take no more than [TIME].
```
