Program Format

How the Program Works

Each week follows a simple two-step flow: pre-study at your own pace, then practice together.

📖

Pre-Study (Before Workshop)

Complete before the live session:

  • 01 Watch the video (5-10 min)
  • 02 Follow along with prompts & templates
  • 03 Try it yourself on your phone or PC
🤝

Workshop (Live Session)

Hands-on building with your peers:

  • 01 Practice together with support
  • 02 Share progress & get peer feedback
  • 03 Complete the weekly Action task

Mindset

Your Builder Mindset

Adopt these four principles and you'll succeed in this program.

You are not here to memorize theory. You are here to build something real that solves a problem for real people.

🚢 Embrace "Good Enough"

Your first app will not be perfect. That's the point. Ship fast, learn fast, improve fast.

🤝 Help Each Other

The best ideas come from collaboration. Share your work, give feedback, and learn from your peers.

📍 Think Local, Act Now

Lagos traffic. Campus frustrations. Market inefficiencies. Your lived experience is your superpower.
