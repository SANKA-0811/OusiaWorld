Video 10 — The Magic Moment

Sheets → AppSheet

This is where it all clicks. One button turns your spreadsheet into a working app.

Step by Step

  • 1 Open your Google Sheet with data
  • 2 Click Extensions in the menu bar
  • 3 Select AppSheet → Create an app
  • 4 Wait 30 seconds...
  • ✨ Your app is alive!
🎉

The "Aha!" Moment

AppSheet automatically creates:

✓ A list view of all your data
✓ A detail view for each item
✓ An input form to add new data
✓ A search function built-in

Workshop Exercise

Build Your App Together

Follow these steps during the workshop. Ask for help if you get stuck!

Part 1: Database (15 min)

  • 1 Use Gemini to design your columns
  • 2 Create a new Google Sheet
  • 3 Add column headers (Row 1)
  • 4 Add 5-10 sample data rows
  • 5 Double-check: no empty rows, no merged cells

Part 2: App Creation (15 min)

  • 6 Extensions → AppSheet → Create an app
  • 7 Explore the auto-generated app
  • 8 Change the app name and icon
  • 9 Pick your brand color
  • 10 Add a camera or GPS input field

Common Mistakes to Avoid:
❌ Empty rows in the middle of your data
❌ Merged cells (AppSheet can't read them)
❌ Column names with special characters
❌ More than one sheet of data (start with one!)

Install on Your Phone!

Your app goes from browser to your phone's home screen.

Android

  • 1 Open your app in AppSheet preview
  • 2 Tap the Share button
  • 3 Select "Add to Home Screen"
  • ✓ It's now on your phone like a real app!

iPhone

  • 1 Open your app link in Safari
  • 2 Tap the Share icon (box with arrow)
  • 3 Scroll down, tap "Add to Home Screen"
  • ✓ Done! Looks like a native app!

You just built an app. Without writing a single line of code.
